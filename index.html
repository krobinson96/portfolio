About

Being born in 1996, some of my first memories are of playing video games on an original PlayStation and typing on an IBM PC shared between my family. After taking specialized college prepatory classes at a magnet high school, I decided to pursue a Bachelor's of Science in Computer Engineering at the University of West Florida. After completing roughly 90 credit hours worth of coursework, I moved to the Evergreen state of Washington in 2016, completing a few credit hours at various local colleges. It was at this time I began writing some of the softwares showcased on my portfolio. While attempting to finish a degree, I began gaining professional experience as a contractor at Boeing as a Field IT Technician. Here I wrote a few proprietary softwares that helped my daily tasks become a little more efficient.

After the contract was completed, I began working as an Electrical Assembler before I was laid off due to the COVID-19 Pandemic and moved to Los Angeles in July of 2020. After some deep thought and careful consideration I have decided to focus more on honing the skills and talents I have gained throughh education and apply them in the real world. Aside from writing softwares, I also create instrumental music through FL Studio and at times, with the help of an AKAI MIDI controller. My cat, Bonkers, is a great source of inspiration for both programming a computer and programming a MPC machine. The softwares that are on display on this website represent the journey I am taking through software engineering, while hoping to capture the process and path I took.

Blackjack

This project is an object-oriented approach at the classic game of Blackjack. At the core of the Blackjack object are Card classes which can hold 13 values of 4 types; constituting cards Ace through King and suits Hearts through Spades. 6 52-card decks are created prior to initiation of the object and then promtly randomly shuffled to simulate playing alone with a 6-shoe deck. A pool of points is prompted for enter, which each round of Blackjack begins with the user setting a wager for the hand. Upon depleting the pool of points, the game is ended and the program is terminated. The game functions as normal, with the dealer standing on 17 and the deck reshuffling upon beginning a round with less than half of live cards remaining.

Backus-Naur Form Tool

A Backus-Naur Form tool, termed Grammar Buddy, can be used to both read and create a completely valid grammar using Backus-Naur form. It uses an object-oriented approach to managing the grammar data structure and maintains a simplistic solution to saving to files. A GUI was developed to handle form management with a graphical representation. To ensure the validity of the form, a number of symbols can be generated so that each generation may be checked individually.
